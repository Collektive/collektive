# What is Collektive?

Collektive is an open-source framework for **aggregate programming** in Kotlin:
it lets you program a whole collective of devices (sensors, robots, smartphones, vehicles, servers)
as a single entity, and takes care of turning that collective program into the local behavior
and the messages of each device.

---

## What problems does Collektive address?

Large-scale distributed systems, such as IoT deployments, robot swarms, or smart-city infrastructures,
are made of many devices that operate autonomously but must collectively achieve global goals,
often without reliable infrastructure, central servers, or stable connectivity.
Programming them device by device forces developers to bridge the gap between local rules and global behavior by hand.

Aggregate programming bridges this gap by construction:
programs describe how *computational fields* (values spread over the network) are created, combined, and evolved,
and the resulting behavior is

- **self-organizing**: global coordination emerges from local interactions, without central control;
- **resilient**: programs keep working, and repair themselves, when devices fail, move, or change their inputs;
- **scalable**: devices only interact with their neighbors, regardless of the size of the system.

See [Building Resilient Systems with Aggregate Programming](../explanation/aggregate-programming.mdx) for an introduction to the paradigm.

## Why Collektive?

<CardGrid columns={2}>
  <Card icon="code" title="Plain, statically typed Kotlin">
    An *internal* DSL: aggregate programs are ordinary Kotlin functions, with full IDE support, static type checking,
    and the whole Kotlin ecosystem. No new language to learn.
  </Card>
  <Card to="/docs/explanation/alignment" icon="sitemap" title="Transparent alignment">
    The compiler plugin matches each message to its operator at compile time, without annotations,
    and warns about common misuses of the DSL while you build.
  </Card>
  <Card to="/docs/explanation/stdlib-descriptions" icon="shield" title="Complete semantic foundation">
    The **eXchange Calculus**, with first-class neighborhood fields, and a standard library of self-stabilizing building blocks:
    distances, broadcasts, collection, gossip, leader election, and time.
  </Card>
  <Card icon="multiplatform" title="Truly multiplatform">
    The same program compiles for the **JVM**, **JavaScript**, and **native** targets, including **Android** and **iOS**:
    devices on different platforms cooperate in the same collective.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Where has Collektive been used?

Collektive has been used to engineer self-organizing spatial structures
(vascular morphogenesis[^1]), runtime replanning in robot swarms, safe swarm control,
distributed particle filtering, and self-stabilizing consensus.
See the [Showcase](/docs/category/showcase/) for the published works, with their experiments and artifacts.

---

[^1]: [A. Cortecchia, D. Pianini, G. Ciatto, and R. Casadei, “An Aggregate Vascular Morphogenesis Controller for Engineered Self-Organising Spatial Structures,” ACSOS 2024](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACSOS61780.2024.00032).
